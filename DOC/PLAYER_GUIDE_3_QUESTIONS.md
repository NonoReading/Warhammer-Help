# Player's guide — 3. "I want to…" (one short sheet per question)

*WFRP4 rules. French version: `GUIDE_JOUEUR_3_QUESTIONS_FR.md`.*

Each sheet answers one question in a few lines. No sheet "change the rules version": a WFRP1 character never becomes WFRP4 (the only exception is WFRP4 ↔ Momentum, below).

## 1. I want to add a weapon, an armour or an item from the books

1. Open the character, go to the equipment table.
2. Click **+Weapon**, **+Armor** or **+Equipment**, then choose in the list.
3. Tick **simplified armour** beforehand if you want simplified armour choices.
4. Click **Save file**.

The list only offers what the character's books contain (see "Use a book that is not ticked").

---

<a id="q2"></a>
## 2. I want to add an item that exists in no book

1. In the equipment table, pick the category (weapon, armour or item) in the category list.
2. Click **+Item**.
3. Fill in the window and confirm.

The item is stored **with the character**, not in a book, so it follows them everywhere.

The window depends on the type of item (same fields as the matching sheet in the book editor):
- **Weapon**: description, skill group, hands, ammunition, encumbrance, price, availability, reach, damage, qualities.
- **Armour**: description, armour points, material, encumbrance, price, availability, locations, qualities.
- **Item** (anything else): description, theme, price, encumbrance, availability, carrying capacity.
- **Quality** (bonus / malus): description, modifier (+ or −), short name, "needs a value" box, explanation.

---

<a id="q3"></a>
## 3. I want to add a free-text line to the equipment

Click **+Text item**, type the wording and the encumbrance, confirm. Useful for a note, a debt or a loose item that needs no rule.

---

<a id="q4"></a>
## 4. I want to give a crafting quality to an item

1. Select the item's line in the equipment table.
2. Click **+Crafting**.
3. Choose the quality in the window: it only offers those that fit the item (weapon, armour or other).

In the window, each quality has a quantity (up to its stated maximum), a type and a short summary; a theme list narrows what is shown. Not available on a spell. The program only **records** the qualities on the item's line: it does not recalculate the price or the encumbrance, so apply the rules by hand.

---

<a id="q5"></a>
## 5. I want to add a familiar or a mount

A familiar is **a character of its own** (species "Familiar") that you created beforehand.

1. Click **+Familiar**.
2. Choose its name in the list of saved familiars.

A line is added to the equipment; it opens the familiar's own sheet. A mount is added the same way (Mounts category). If the list is empty, create the familiar first (see the creation guide, extra step "Familiar form"). The button only appears in the Mounts category.

---

<a id="q6"></a>
## 6. I want to record a mutation

1. Go to the **Corruption** page.
2. Click **Add** and enter the corruption points gained.
3. After a failed Corruption test, click **Mutation** and choose from the list.
4. If the character has Resilience left, one of the choices lets them **spend Resilience instead**.

The mutation then appears on the **Mutations** page, and the talents it gives or removes are tracked.

---

<a id="q7"></a>
## 7. I want to record an affliction or a disease

On the **Afflictions / Diseases** page, click **Add an Affliction** or **Add a Disease** and choose from the catalogue.

If an Affliction has a random penalty (for example "−1d10 Toughness"), the program shows the roll and **you confirm or correct it**. Cancel to cancel the addition.

---

<a id="q8"></a>
## 8. I want to use the Momentum talents (WFRP4 → WFRP5)

1. Check that the **Momentum** book is among the character's books. Otherwise a message tells you it is not.
2. On the **Total experience** page, tick **Opt : Momentum talents (WFRP5 rules)**.
3. Click **Save file**.

On saving, the character's Rulebook talents are replaced by their Momentum versions (15 talents), the choice lists are filtered accordingly, and a career change is converted too. Untick to go back. A Momentum talent with no WFRP4 equivalent stays as it is.

---

<a id="q9"></a>
## 9. I want to use a book that is not ticked

The books are chosen at creation (step 0) and stored with the character. To use another book afterwards, open the **Book** page of the character window and tick the books the character may use. Without that, its species, careers, talents and equipment are not offered.

---

<a id="q10"></a>
## 10. I want to make my own house rules or my own data

The program does not lock what comes from a non-official book: **everything is editable** there. Official books are read-only.

- To change a rule, a career or an item, use the book editor (WinLivre). Official books are read-only there; the exception is the **personal book**, a book where you can add any element you want.
- For one character only, use **+Item** or **+Text item** (sheets 2 and 3).

---

<a id="q11"></a>
## 11. I want a PDF sheet that suits my printer

Under the **PDF** button, tick:

- **Ink saving**: outlines only;
- **Colour** unticked: greyscale;
- **Two-page PDF (Feldo)** for the two-page sheet, with **Warhammer style** for red banners and parchment lines;
- **Without asterisks** or **Without amount** to lighten the sheet.

Then click **PDF**: the sheet uses the values shown on screen.

---

<a id="q12"></a>
## 12. I want to go back to an earlier state of the character

On the **History** page, select the line of the state you want and click the **See** button: the sheet shows the character as it was then, in read-only view. It is only for looking: you **cannot go back** to that state.
