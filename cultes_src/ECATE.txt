gift 1: Gain an animal-spirit familiar as a companion. Upon becoming a Priest 1st Rank the Master is granted a dream vision in which a spirit-creature comes to him; roll once on the Ecate Familiar Table to discover its species. The familiar becomes the character's constant companion and confers advantages in keeping with its nature, while the character must honor certain strictures. The character is also assured of a friendly, or at least neutral, response from all encountered animals of the familiar's species. Use the evil eye ability as the Level 2 Necromantic spell evil eye once per day at no magic point cost, used like a divine prayer (no reagents); the effect continues until the subject makes a successful WP test, and moving beyond four yards from the priest does not end it.
gift 2: Speak with animals as through a gift of tongues prayer once a day as an ability. Gain the hex ability as the minor curse prayer, with unlimited range so long as the Master has made an effigy or doll using a sample of the victim's nails, hair, blood, urine or excrement; the curse is an uncomfortable, unsightly, perhaps painful but not especially dangerous affliction (warts, boils, baldness, bad breath, etc.).
gift 3: Use the transvection ability as the Level 1 Battle spell flight minor once per day, no magic point cost, duration 1 hour. Use the dream sending ability as the Level 3 Illusionist ritual dream sending once per day at no magic point cost, used like a divine prayer, with unlimited range so long as the Master has made an effigy or doll using a sample of the victim's nails, hair, blood, urine or excrement.
gift 4: Use the weather control ability as the amulet of thunderhead once per day.
