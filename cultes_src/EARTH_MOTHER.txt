gift 1: Gain an animal-spirit familiar as a companion. Upon becoming a Priest 1st Rank the druid is granted a dream vision in which a spirit-creature comes to him; roll once on the Old Faith Familiar Table to discover its species. The familiar becomes the character's constant companion and confers advantages in keeping with its nature, while the character must honor certain strictures. The character is also assured of a friendly, or at least neutral, response from all encountered animals of the familiar's species.
